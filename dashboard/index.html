Decision Support & Revenue Analytics

Two years of transactions from a UK online giftware retailer, turned into the questions a commercial team actually asks: who are our best customers, do new customers come back, which markets and products carry the business, and what should we expect next quarter?

Public dataset, not a client project. Built on UCI Online Retail II (CC BY 4.0). Every figure below is computed from that data by the open-source pipeline in this repository. Amounts are in GBP, 1 Dec 2009 – 9 Dec 2011.

Data & method

Every cleaning rule is a tested dbt model. This is the audit trail from raw lines to the numbers above.

What “revenue” means here. Net revenue = product sales − product cancellations, at the prices on the invoice. Postage, marketplace fees, bank charges, manual adjustments, vouchers and test lines are excluded. The dataset has no cost data, so no margin is shown.

Pipeline. Python ingestion → DuckDB → dbt (staging → intermediate → marts, 85 tests) → pre-aggregated JSON → this static page. The dbt models use cross-database macros and ship with an optional BigQuery profile; CI runs them on DuckDB.
